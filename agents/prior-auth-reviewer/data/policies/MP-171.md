# MP-171: MRI of the knee

Procedure codes: 73721, 73722, 73723
Body region: knee, lower extremity joint
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers MRI of the knee for suspected internal derangement (meniscus or ligament injury).

## Coverage criteria
All of K1 to K3 must be met, unless a red flag applies.

### K1. Duration of symptoms
Knee pain for at least 4 weeks.

### K2. Plain X-ray first
Plain X-rays of the knee were completed and did not explain the symptoms.

### K3. Mechanical symptoms
Locking, catching or giving way is documented, or an exam finding suggests a ligament tear.

## Red flags
### RF. Red flags that waive K1
Suspected fracture not seen on X-ray, suspected infection, or a locked knee that cannot extend.

## Exclusions
Knee MRI for osteoarthritis already shown on X-ray is not covered.
