# MP-117: MRI of the lumbar spine

Procedure codes: 72148, 72149, 72158
Body region: lumbar spine, lower back
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine, with or without contrast, for adults with
low back pain or lumbar radicular symptoms in the outpatient setting.

## Definitions
Conservative therapy means physical therapy, a home exercise program supervised by a clinician, prescription or
over-the-counter anti-inflammatory medication, or a combination of these. A neurological exam documents strength,
reflexes and sensation in the lower extremities.

## Coverage criteria
All of C1 to C4 must be met, unless a red flag applies.

### C1. Duration of symptoms
Low back pain for at least 6 weeks.

### C2. Conservative therapy
At least 6 weeks of conservative therapy (physical therapy, medication or both) that did not resolve symptoms.

### C3. Neurological exam
A neurological exam is documented in the clinical notes.

### C4. No recent lumbar MRI
No MRI of the lumbar spine in the past 12 months. Imaging of other body regions does not count.

## Red flags
### RF. Red flags that waive C1 and C2
Progressive neurological deficit, suspected cauda equina syndrome, or a history of cancer with new back pain waive
C1 and C2. C3 and C4 still apply.

## Exclusions
Routine imaging for acute low back pain under 6 weeks without red flags is not covered. Repeat imaging for
unchanged chronic pain is not covered.

## Documentation required
Clinical notes stating symptom duration, therapies tried and their dates, the neurological exam, and any prior
spine imaging with its date and body region.
