# MP-119: CT of the lumbar spine

Procedure codes: 72131, 72132, 72133
Body region: lumbar spine, lower back
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers computed tomography (CT) of the lumbar spine when MRI cannot be performed.

## Coverage criteria
Both T1 and T2 must be met.

### T1. MRI contraindicated
A documented contraindication to MRI, such as a non-conditional pacemaker, implanted metal or severe claustrophobia
not manageable with sedation.

### T2. Lumbar MRI criteria otherwise met
The request would meet the criteria of MP-117 (duration, conservative therapy, neurological exam) if MRI were possible.

## Exclusions
CT is not covered as a first choice when MRI is possible.
