# MP-118: MRI of the cervical spine

Procedure codes: 72141, 72142, 72156
Body region: cervical spine, neck
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers MRI of the cervical spine for adults with neck pain or cervical radicular symptoms.

## Coverage criteria
All of N1 to N4 must be met, unless a red flag applies.

### N1. Duration of symptoms
Neck pain or arm radicular symptoms for at least 6 weeks.

### N2. Conservative therapy
At least 6 weeks of physical therapy or medication without resolution of neck symptoms.

### N3. Neurological exam
Upper extremity strength, reflexes and sensation are documented.

### N4. No recent cervical MRI
No MRI of the cervical spine in the past 12 months.

## Red flags
### RF. Red flags that waive N1 and N2
Signs of cervical myelopathy, significant trauma, or a history of cancer with new neck pain.

## Exclusions
Imaging for uncomplicated neck strain under 6 weeks is not covered.
