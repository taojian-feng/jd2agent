# MP-172: MRI of the shoulder

Procedure codes: 73221, 73222, 73223
Body region: shoulder, upper extremity joint
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers MRI of the shoulder for suspected rotator cuff tear or labral injury.

## Coverage criteria
All of S1 to S3 must be met, unless a red flag applies.

### S1. Duration of symptoms
Shoulder pain for at least 6 weeks.

### S2. Plain X-ray first
Plain X-rays of the shoulder were completed.

### S3. Physical therapy
At least 4 weeks of physical therapy without improvement.

## Red flags
### RF. Red flags that waive S1 and S3
Acute trauma with suspected full-thickness rotator cuff tear and weakness on exam.
