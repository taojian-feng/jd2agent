# MP-305: Outpatient physical therapy beyond 12 visits

Procedure codes: 97110, 97112, 97140
Body region: any; rehabilitation services
Effective: 2026-07-01 (fictional policy for demonstration)

## Scope
This policy covers outpatient physical therapy visits after the first 12 visits in a plan year.

## Coverage criteria
All of P1 to P3 must be met.

### P1. Functional goals
The plan of care states measurable functional goals.

### P2. Measurable progress
Progress toward the goals is documented with objective measures since the last authorization.

### P3. Signed plan of care
The plan of care is signed by the treating clinician within the last 90 days.

## Exclusions
Maintenance therapy without expected functional improvement is not covered.
