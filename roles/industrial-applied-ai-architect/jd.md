# Senior Principal Architect, Applied AI (Enterprise)

_Example input for JD2Agent. A generic posting for a large industrial equipment manufacturer, adapted from real

## Job Summary

Join the enterprise AI team and help shape how the company builds and deploys AI-powered solutions across the
enterprise. As a senior AI architect, you'll serve as a technical leader and trusted advisor on AI solution design,
with a focus on agentic systems that can reason, plan and take action. You'll partner with IT, business functions
and product teams to ensure AI solutions are architecturally sound, scalable and aligned with where the industry is
heading. This is a high-visibility role with direct impact on the company's AI strategy and hands-on work with
emerging technologies.

## What You Will Do

1. Develop detailed architecture deliverables that solve business problems through agentic AI systems and patterns.
2. Design an AI application's technical infrastructure, including orchestration layers, tool integrations, data
   retrieval patterns and testing approaches.
3. Lead the evaluation and deployment of emerging AI technologies and frameworks to grow the company's agentic
   capabilities.
4. Translate business requirements into AI solution designs and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver
   results.

## What You Will Have

- **Effective Communications:** transmit, receive and accurately interpret ideas, information and needs.
- **AI/ML Architecture:** deep understanding of transformer models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agent
  frameworks and model orchestration patterns.
- **Analytical Thinking:** determine the root cause of organizational problems and create alternative solutions.
- **Cloud AI Platforms:** hands-on experience with major cloud AI platforms (Azure OpenAI Service, AWS Bedrock,
  Google Vertex AI) and the ability to architect multi-cloud or hybrid solutions.
- **Requirements Analysis:** elicit, analyze and record functional and non-functional requirements.
- **Data Architecture:** design blueprints for integrating data resources for business processes.
- **Target Architecture:** develop the IT blueprint and roadmap, aligned with business strategy.
- **MLOps:** model versioning, deployment pipelines, monitoring and governance frameworks.

## Considerations For Top Candidates

- Deep experience designing and building agentic AI systems, including multi-agent architectures, tool use and
  orchestration patterns
- Production AI agent systems built with LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel or similar orchestration tools
- Strong understanding of LLM capabilities, limitations and deployment patterns (prompt engineering, RAG, function
  calling, context engineering)
- Experience evaluating and integrating AI/ML infrastructure (vector databases, embedding models, orchestration
  layers, observability tools)
- Broad software engineering background, including API design, authentication and authorization patterns, and
  enterprise integration
- Ability to translate complex technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders and influence architecture
  decisions across teams
- A record of staying current with the AI landscape and bringing practical recommendations
- Experience with MLOps/LLMOps practices, model monitoring, or production AI systems at scale
