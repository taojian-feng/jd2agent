## Summary

Help leading enterprise software vendors (ISVs) design, build, optimize and deploy production-grade agentic AI
systems on an accelerated computing platform. Partner with strategic software companies to turn frontier AI
capabilities into reliable enterprise products, spanning multi-agent orchestration, RAG, tool use, model
customization and production deployment.

The team works where partner engineering, the platform and product development meet. You lead technical projects
from first exploration through architecture, prototyping, performance optimization, production rollout and field
enablement, helping partners adopt the platform and GPU-accelerated infrastructure.

## What You'll Be Doing

1. Lead technical delivery for strategic agentic AI partner engagements, from discovery and architecture through PoC, production readiness, rollout and scale.
2. Design and build enterprise-grade agentic systems, including multi-agent workflows, tool-using agents, RAG-integrated applications, planning, memory, evaluation and guardrail patterns.
3. Lead deep architecture reviews with partner engineering teams, driving trade-offs across model quality, latency, efficiency, cost, retrieval quality, reliability, safety, security and observability.
4. Build hands-on PoCs, benchmarks, reference architectures and reusable blueprints that help enterprise ISVs and field teams move from exploration to production.
5. Guide partners on model customization and post-training workflows, including supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning methods, human or AI feedback, direct preference optimization, PEFT/LoRA, synthetic data generation, evaluation and regression analysis.
6. Work with agent harnesses and execution environments such as sandboxed agent shells, OpenAI Agents SDK, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, LangChain, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel or similar frameworks.
7. Translate partner deployment findings into actionable feedback for product and engineering, to improve the platform, tools and field guidance.

## What We Need To See

1. BS/MS/PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, AI/ML, or equivalent experience.
2. 8+ years of engineering, solutions architecture, applied ML or technical deployment experience.
3. A consistent track record leading complex AI, ML, distributed systems or enterprise software deployments from prototype to production.
4. Hands-on experience building LLM, generative AI, RAG or agentic AI applications in production or production-like environments.
5. Strong programming and debugging skills in Python and Linux, with experience in PyTorch, TensorFlow or similar deep learning frameworks.
6. Deep understanding of agentic AI architectures, including tool use, orchestration, memory, retrieval, planning, evaluation, guardrails and failure handling.
7. Experience with model customization or post-training techniques such as SFT, RL/RLHF/RLAIF, DPO, reward modeling, LoRA/PEFT, quantization-aware optimization or model evaluation.
8. Ability to lead ambiguous partner engagements, influence senior engineering collaborators, and communicate clearly with technical and executive audiences.

## Ways To Stand Out

1. Hands-on experience with the platform's AI software: inference microservices, a model customization framework, retrieval microservices, a guardrails toolkit, an agent toolkit, distributed inference serving, open reasoning models, an inference server and an LLM inference optimizer.
2. Experience building post-training pipelines for reasoning, tool use, domain adaptation, enterprise task performance or agent behavior improvement.
3. Experience with agent harnesses, sandboxed execution, policy enforcement, sandboxed-shell-like environments, or building a secure enterprise agent runtime.
4. Recognized expertise in RAG, model customization, agent orchestration, enterprise AI security or GPU-accelerated AI infrastructure, with field-facing technical presence through workshops, architecture reviews, talks, whitepapers or developer enablement.
